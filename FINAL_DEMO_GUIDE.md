# AI Research Studio — Finals Demo Guide

1. Open the Workspace and select **Load demo brief**.
2. Generate four personas; the app works with local, deterministic fallback data if no Gemini key is present.
3. Open Persona Cards to show quality, realism and consistency signals, then use gallery/list and exports.
4. Run a survey template, ask an interview question, and moderate a Focus Group prompt.
5. Extract Insights to show themes, sentiment, confidence, risks and recommendations.
6. Generate the Product Consultant recommendation and finish on Dashboard with the PDF report.

Judge-friendly message: AI Research Studio takes a product team from an ambiguous brief to explainable, evidence-shaped launch decisions in one guided workspace.
