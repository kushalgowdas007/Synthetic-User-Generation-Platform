# Behavior Modeling

## Introduction

Behavior modeling is the process of predicting how a synthetic user behaves based on demographic information, interests, profession, and personality.

## Purpose

The objective is to simulate realistic human behaviour for research and experimentation.

## Behaviour Factors

- Age
- Gender
- Profession
- Location
- Interests
- Personality
- Lifestyle

## Behaviour Categories

### Purchasing Behaviour

Represents how users make buying decisions.

### Learning Behaviour

Shows how users acquire knowledge and explore new topics.

### Social Behaviour

Represents communication style and interaction with others.

### Technology Usage

Shows how frequently users adopt digital technologies.

### Decision Making

Models whether the user is analytical, emotional, or balanced.

## Benefits

- Realistic simulations
- Better UX testing
- Improved product validation
- Supports AI research

## Future Improvements

- Emotional state modelling
