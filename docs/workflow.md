# Workflow Explanation

## Step 1

User enters experiment details.

---

## Step 2

Workspace validates the information.

---

## Step 3

Persona Generation Agent sends prompts to Google Gemini.

---

## Step 4

Gemini returns structured persona information.

---

## Step 5

Behavior Simulation enriches the persona.

---

## Step 6

Persona Cards are generated.

---

## Step 7

Conversation history is stored inside Memory Store.

---

## Step 8

Consistency Checker validates all responses.

---

## Step 9

Survey Agent generates responses.

---

## Step 10

Responses are analyzed for future analytics.
